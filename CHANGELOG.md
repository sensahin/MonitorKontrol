# Changelog

All notable changes to MonitorKontrol are documented here.

## 1.0.0 - 2026-08-24

- Initial open-source release.
- Independent brightness control for built-in and external displays.
- Hardware DDC/CI brightness, contrast, and volume with read-only capability
  probing.
- Native MacBook backlight control and explicit software-dimming fallback.
- Combined brightness control, quick scenes, saved scenes, diagnostics, and
  hot-plug/wake rediscovery.
- Launch-at-login and external-display-only menu-bar visibility options.
